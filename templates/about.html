Project Overview

Multilingual AI Smart Meeting Recorder & Intelligent Meeting Assistant

Purpose & Scope

A production-ready meeting intelligence web application engineered to eliminate language barriers in corporate, educational, and cross-cultural team meetings. It provides native-script preservation for Indian languages (Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Bengali, etc.), on-demand translation, executive summarization, action item extraction, priority-based email drafting, and Unicode PDF report generation.

Key Capabilities

  • Zero Language Assumptions: Automatically detects spoken language without forcing English transcription.
  • Dual-Track Translation: Keeps original transcript intact while offering side-by-side translation.
  • Action Item Extraction: Automatically isolates tasks, owners, deadlines, and urgency ratings.
  • Email Assistant: Automatically assigns Priority tiers (Urgent, High, Medium, Low) and crafts follow-ups.
  • Flawless Unicode PDF: Embedded Google Noto TrueType fonts ensure zero broken glyph boxes (□□□) on Render.

Six-Stage Interconnected Intelligence Pipeline

01

Audio Recording

Captures live browser microphone audio with real-time waveform visualization, saved to meetings/meeting.wav.

02

Multilingual Speech-to-Text

Groq Whisper auto-detects spoken languages and transcribes native text into meetings/transcript.txt.

03

AI Translation Engine

Translates full transcripts into English or other languages, saved to meetings/translated_transcript.txt.

04

Summarization & Actions

Condenses discussions into 7 executive sections and structured action item tables in meetings/summary.txt.

05

Email Drafting Assistant

Evaluates urgency, assigns Priority tags, and drafts follow-up emails in meetings/email_draft.txt.

06

Unicode PDF Engine

Compiles intelligence into print-ready Unicode PDFs with Google Noto fonts at meetings/Meeting_Report.pdf.
